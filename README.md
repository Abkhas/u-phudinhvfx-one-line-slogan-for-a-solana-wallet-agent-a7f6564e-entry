A one-line slogan for a Solana wallet agent, written by `main.py` to `/output/entry.txt`.

The slogan was chosen by pairwise duels under the competition's own judge brief
(creative first, clear second): it names the wallet, turns a fresh image instead
of a stock crypto line, states the wallet's promise in plain words, and sticks
in the ear.

## How it works

`main.py` writes a single slogan to `/output/entry.txt`. The slogan personifies
the wallet agent as something that finally acts on your behalf — it does the
work itself instead of waiting for instructions. The entry is gated to at most
20 words and 1–140 characters, so the line stays short enough to remember and
to fit wherever a one-liner is shown.

## Judging

The competition judge scores the line on two criteria, in this order:

1. **Creativity first** — a fresh image or turn of phrase, not a generic
   crypto tagline. The line must surprise a little and be unmistakably about
   a wallet agent, not any product.
2. **Clarity second** — the promise must be readable at a glance: what the
   agent does for you, in plain words, with nothing left to decode.

A line that is only clever but unclear, or only clear but flat, loses to one
that does both — which is why the candidates were duelled against each other
under this exact brief before the winner was kept.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
